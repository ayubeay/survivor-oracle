# 0.4.3 - Holder concentration denominator correction

**Date:** 2026-07-29 · **Scope:** affected the holder-concentration signal on every score issued

## The bug
getHolderDistribution computed:

    top10Percent = sum(top 10 accounts) / sum(top 20 accounts)

That is a real statistic - how dominant the largest ten token accounts are relative to the
largest twenty. The fault was not the arithmetic itself but that everything downstream
treated it as a different measurement:

- the field was named top10HolderPercent
- scoreHolderConcentration applied thresholds assuming a fraction of TOTAL SUPPLY
  (<=20% scores 100, >=85% scores 10)
- /report presented it to paying customers as holder concentration

The implementation measured one thing while the interface and scoring semantics claimed
it measured another. Because the top ten accounts always dominate accounts eleven through
twenty, the ratio sat in the high bands for tokens whose actual supply concentration was
moderate.

## Measured impact
| token | reported (top10/top20) | actual (top10/supply) | sub-score | final score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BONK | 70.78% | 37.77% | 20 -> 60 | 61 -> 67 |
| JUP | 91.43% | 66.78% | 10 -> 40 | 59 -> 64 |

BONK's gate decision changed from CHALLENGE to ALLOW.

## Fix
getHolderDistribution returns raw base-unit sums (top10RawUnits, sampledRawUnits).
fetchTokenData computes the percentage against mint supply once both RPC results are in
hand, preserving the parallel Promise.all rather than sequencing the calls.

Units verified before changing anything: getTokenLargestAccounts amounts and
getParsedAccountInfo supply are both raw base units, and top20 <= supply held on both
test tokens.

concentrationBasis is exposed in signals: denominator, supply_raw, accounts_sampled,
sampled_raw_units.

## Also fixed
Zero-account holder queries previously returned top10HolderPercent: 100 - asserting total
concentration having measured nothing. Now returns null with NO_HOLDER_ACCOUNTS_RETURNED,
routing to the unknown state (sub-score 30, the missing-data default) rather than scoring
as though measured and terrible.

## Known remaining limitations (stated in /report)
- counts token accounts, not beneficial owners
- program-owned pools, staking vaults and exchange custody are included
- denominator is total supply, which includes burned and locked tokens

Distinguishing token accounts from beneficial owners is a substantially harder problem
than correcting a denominator. It deserves its own design, its own receipt fields, and its
own version bump - deliberately not attempted in 0.4.3.

## Traceability
Scores from 0.4.2 and earlier are not comparable to 0.4.3 on this signal. Receipts carry
scoring_version, so any prior receipt remains interpretable against the version that
produced it.
